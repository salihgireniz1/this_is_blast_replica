# Performance pass

The ledger for the measured pass. Every step here is one change, one reason, and the
numbers before and after it. The case study's performance section is written from this
file; nothing is claimed that is not in a table below.

## Targets

| | Target |
|---|---|
| Frame time on the reference phone | under 16.6 ms at 60 Hz, aiming for the 8.33 ms budget from `PLAN.md` |
| Managed allocation during gameplay | 0 B per frame |
| Worst case scene | `Level_03`: 900 cubes (10x30x3), 100 shooters, five slots firing |

`Level_03` exists for this file alone. It is generated by `Docs/Tools/generate_level_03.py`:
ten queue columns of one colour each, two per colour, so every colour is always at a front
and the level is winnable by construction; twelve hidden shooters; ammo equals cubes. It is
the third entry of the scope's level list and nothing boots it by default: the game starts
on `Level_01`. To play it, set the saved level index to 2 (`ES3.Save<int>("LevelIndex", 2)`
in the editor; on the phone, push a `SaveFile.es3` holding that key).

## Reference device

Samsung Galaxy A16 (SM-A165F): MediaTek Helio G99, Mali-G57 MC2, 6 GB RAM, 1080x2340,
60/90 Hz, Android 16. A budget phone of 2024; if it holds 60 fps, a weaker device holds 30.

## How it is measured

`PerfProbe` (Presentation) is a MonoBehaviour that reads Unity's own counters through
`ProfilerRecorder` and logs one line per second: mean and worst frame time, GC bytes and
allocation count per frame, batches, SetPass calls, draw calls, shadow casters, triangles.
It runs only where `Debug.isDebugBuild` is true (the editor and development builds), so a
release build carries the component but never ticks. On the phone the lines come out of
`adb logcat`; in the editor, out of the console. The same probe measures both, so the two
columns of every table below are comparable.

Editor numbers are honest for allocations, batches and SetPass (those are the engine's
counts), and dishonest for milliseconds (a desktop GPU and CPU). Milliseconds are read on
the phone only.

## Plan

Each step is measured before and after. A step that does not move a number is reverted
and recorded as such.

1. Baseline: the probe in the scene, `Level_02` with five slots firing, editor and phone.
2. Free wins, settings only: `targetFrameRate`, splash shadows and particle cap, shadow
   distance and resolution sized to the board, Animator culling for off-screen shooters.
3. Allocations: whatever the probe shows, closed one source at a time.
4. Draw calls: Frame Debugger count; SRP Batcher compatibility of `CustomShader`; the
   outline's second material; unused shader keywords.
5. The one quality trade: bloom. Measured, then decided by the number.
6. A low quality tier, only if 1-5 leave the phone under 60.

## Ledger

### 1. Baseline

Development build, Vulkan, nothing changed yet. "Firing" = the five front shooters seated,
every slot shooting. One probe line per second; the numbers are typical lines, not averages.

Phone, `Level_01` (100 cubes, 15 shooters):

| State | fps | frame ms | main ms | GC B/frame | batches | SetPass | draws | shadow casters | tris |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| idle, nobody seated | 30.0 | 33.34 | 33.1 | 16 (x1) | 311 | 11 | 312 | 143 | 111.7k |
| firing, 50 cubes gone | 30.0 | 33.33 | 33.3 | 16 (x1) | 186 | 11 | 187 | 83 | 64.5k |

Phone, `Level_03` (900 cubes, 100 shooters; the board runs off the top of the frame, so
what is drawn is what the frustum keeps):

| State | fps | frame ms | worst ms | main ms | GC B/frame | batches | SetPass | draws | shadow casters | tris |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| idle | 27.5 | 36.3 | 50.0 | 35.9 | 16 (x1) | 972 | 14 | 973 | 503 | 372.1k |
| firing | 27.5 | 36.3 | 50.0 | 36.4 | 78 (x1) | 977 | 14 | 978 | 505 | 373.6k |

The stress level is over budget before anyone taps: the frame is 36 ms with a 33 ms cap,
so every fourth frame is dropped. Firing changes nothing visible in the render counters
because dying front rows pull back rows into the frustum; the draw count is the frustum's,
not the level's. Where the 36 ms goes (CPU submit of ~980 draws, or the GPU's two passes
over 373k triangles plus MSAA and bloom at 1080x2340) is what step 2 separates.

What the first table already says:

- **The game runs at 30 fps.** `targetFrameRate` is -1 and Android's default is 30, so
  every frame is exactly one 33.3 ms vsync slot. The main thread number includes the wait
  for that slot, so the real per-frame cost is hidden under the cap; it shows only once the
  cap is lifted (step 2).
- **One 16-byte allocation every frame, idle or firing.** Not the game's tweens (those run
  only while firing); something in the standing scene allocates once per frame. Hunted in
  step 3.
- **Every cube costs two draws**: one in the main pass and one into the shadow map. Fifty
  cubes dying took 125 batches and 60 shadow casters with them.
- SetPass stays at 11 whatever the cube count: the SRP Batcher is doing its job and
  materials are not the problem.
